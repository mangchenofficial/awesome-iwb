### Rand 抽号器

<img src="./images/rand-icon.png" height="56" width="56"/>

![新人出道](https://img.shields.io/badge/新人出道-blue)
[![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/LuoYunXi0407/Rand?style=flat&color=red)](https://github.com/LuoYunXi0407/Rand/stargazers)
[![GitHub License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/LuoYunXi0407/Rand?color=red)](https://github.com/LuoYunXi0407/Rand)

Rand 是一款简洁、现代化、易用的抽号软件，专为触摸屏设备和 Windows 7 及以上版本操作系统设计，采用 Fluent Design 风格，界面美观且易于操作。支持单人和多人模式，保证抽号过程不重复。

🏷 **关键词**：<br/>
![随机点名](https://img.shields.io/badge/随机点名-white)
![抽号](https://img.shields.io/badge/抽号-white)
![Fluent Design](https://img.shields.io/badge/Fluent_Design-white)

### IslandCaller

<img src="./images/islandcaller-icon.png" height="56" width="56"/>

![新人出道](https://img.shields.io/badge/新人出道-blue)
[![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/HUSX100/IslandCaller?style=flat&color=red)](https://github.com/HUSX100/IslandCaller/stargazers)
[![GitHub License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/HUSX100/IslandCaller?color=red)](https://github.com/HUSX100/IslandCaller)

IslandCaller 是一个基于 ClassIsland 提醒服务的轻量级点名器，用于在课堂上快速点名。通过注册 URL 协议来创建桌面快捷方式或浏览器书签实现快速点名。

🏷 **关键词**：<br/>
![随机点名](https://img.shields.io/badge/随机点名-white)
![ClassIsland](https://img.shields.io/badge/ClassIsland-white)

💬 **Douxiba 锐评**：目前来说真的很"轻量"，编辑名单的体验并不是很好。作为 ClassIsland 插件来说安装方便，但部分功能有待加强。
